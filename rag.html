What is RAG?

Retrieval-Augmented Generation gives a model relevant evidence from an external knowledge source before it answers.

Detail

A RAG system chunks and indexes content, retrieves relevant passages, and supplies them to the model as context.

Retrieve first, generate second.

Sample

An operations assistant retrieves the latest settlement policy and cites it before answering.
