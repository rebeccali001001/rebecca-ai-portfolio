14 · AI Engineering & Operations

Make AI Systems Understandable

AI engineering turns models, prompts, retrieval, tools, and agents into reliable systems. Operations makes those systems understandable after they meet real users.

Start with the signals that explain what happened, where time went, and what to improve next.

Module takeaway
Good AI operations turns a black box into a system whose behavior can be explained, measured, and improved.
