01 · AI Foundations · Topic 05

What is Generative AI?

Generative AI creates new text, code, images, audio, video, or structured outputs from learned patterns. The useful unit is the complete workflow around generation.

Easy analogy

Generative AI is a creative studio: a prompt becomes text, images, code, or audio, with a human reviewer in the loop.

Analogy video

Definition

A generative model produces a new response from an input and context. It can be fluent and useful, but fluency alone does not prove that the result is true.

In plain language

Ask for a new draft, then supply facts, check the result, and decide whether a person or system should act on it.

Key points

  • Many formats: text, code, images, audio, video, and structured data.
  • Grounding: trusted context helps with factual work.
  • Validation: shape and policy checks catch unsafe or unusable output.
  • Handoff: approval belongs in the workflow when risk is material.

Sample · drafting a recipe

Imagine a kitchen helper drafting a recipe from today’s ingredients. It proposes; the surrounding workflow makes the result trustworthy enough to use.

GenerateDraft a recipeThe model creates a new answer from learned patterns.
GroundAdd real factsInventory, dietary rules, or a source document provide context.
ValidateCheck the draftFormat, required details, and policy constraints are tested.
ApproveConfirm the dishA person or system takes the final action when appropriate.

Where it is used

Writing assistance, coding copilots, image and video creation, meeting summaries, document drafting, synthetic data, and conversational interfaces.

Key takeaway

Generative AI can create fluently; a safe workflow makes facts, checks, and handoff visible.
