What is Video Generation?

Video generation uses AI models to create or transform sequences of visual frames, often with motion and sometimes audio.

What is it?

Video generation is the use of AI models to create or transform moving visual content.

Inputs can include text, images, video, motion references, or other conditions. A useful result must model appearance, motion, and consistency across time.

Think of it like...

Think of it like creating a moving storyboard where each moment must look good and stay consistent with what came before.

How it works

The system turns conditions into a time-aware visual representation, then renders and decodes a sequence of frames.

1 · InputProvide conditionsText, an image, video, motion reference, or another signal arrives.
2 · EncodeRepresent the conditionsThe inputs become signals the model can use.
3 · GenerateModel time and appearanceA generative video model builds a temporal visual representation.
4 · Create framesMake the sequenceThe model produces frames that should connect smoothly over time.
5 · DecodeAssemble the videoThe representation is decoded into viewable video frames.
6 · OutputReview the resultThe final video can be watched, edited, or passed to another workflow.

Real-world examples

Text-to-video

Input: A scene description

Output: A short moving clip

Image-to-video

Input: A still image

Output: An animated version of the scene

Marketing and previsualization

Input: A brief, storyboard, or motion reference

Output: A draft clip for communication or planning

What it is NOT

Video Generation ≠ Image Generation Repeated Independently

Video Generation: Models consistency across time.

Independent images: Each image can change without matching the previous one.

Video Generation ≠ Video Editing

Video Generation: Creates or transforms visual sequences with a generative model.

Video Editing: Arranges or changes existing footage.

Video Generation ≠ One Specific Model Architecture

Video Generation: A capability or task.

Model architecture: One possible design used to build that capability.

Related concepts

Input conditions → Generative video model → Frames over time
Video Generation is a modality-specific generation task. It shares ideas with Image Generation while adding temporal consistency.
