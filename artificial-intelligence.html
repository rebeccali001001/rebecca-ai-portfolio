01 · AI Foundations · Topic 01

What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence is the field of building systems that perform tasks associated with human intelligence, such as recognizing patterns, understanding language, making predictions, or taking actions.

Easy analogy

AI as a capable assistant connecting perception, language, and decisions to help a person complete a task.

Analogy video

Definition

AI is an umbrella term. A system can use rules, data, statistical models, or learned representations to solve a defined task.

In plain language

Give a computer a job that usually needs judgment, then design a system that turns input into a helpful output.

Key points

  • Task-specific: capable at a job does not mean human-like in general.
  • Evidence matters: measure whether the output is useful and correct.
  • Human-aware: keep review and escalation where mistakes matter.

Sample · a kitchen helper

Imagine a robot helping in a kitchen. AI is the complete system that turns a person’s request into a safe next step.

Input“Make dinner”A request, image, sensor reading, or document comes in.
UnderstandFind the taskThe system identifies what the person needs.
DecideChoose a responseRules or models produce a recommendation or action.
ReviewAsk when unsureA person can confirm, correct, or take over.

Where it is used

Document extraction, fraud detection, search ranking, recommendations, accessibility tools, medical-image support, and customer-service triage.

Key takeaway

The practical question is not “Is this AI?” but “What task, what evidence, and where does a human stay in the loop?”
