Training & Model Adaptation · Glossary

Topic Glossary

Plain-English definitions for the concepts in this module, including the new Synthetic Data topic.

TermSimple EnglishPM-friendly meaning
Training DataExamples a model learns from.The material that shapes learned patterns and behavior.
Synthetic DataData generated by rules, simulation, or an AI model.A scalable, controlled input for training, evaluation, or testing—if quality is checked.
Pre-trainingBroad initial training on large amounts of data.The stage that creates general capabilities in a base model.
Fine-tuningAdditional training on focused examples.A way to adapt a pre-trained model to a more specific task or behavior.
Instruction TuningTraining on instructions paired with desired responses.Helps a model follow task instructions more consistently.
DistillationUsing a larger or stronger model to teach a smaller model.A way to transfer useful behavior into a cheaper or faster model.
Data AugmentationCreating variations of existing examples.Expands coverage while trying to preserve the original task meaning.
Evaluation DatasetA set of cases used to measure performance.Evidence for deciding whether a model or system is good enough.
Model CollapseLoss of useful variation after repeated training on generated data.A risk when synthetic outputs replace too much real-world diversity.

Synthetic Data is related to training data, but the terms are not identical: synthetic data describes where examples came from; training data describes how examples are used.
