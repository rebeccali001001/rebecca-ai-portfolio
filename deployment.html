What Is AI Deployment?

Deployment is the process of releasing an AI system into a real environment where users or applications can access it.

Deployment turns a tested system into an operational service or workflow.

What is it?

Deployment is the controlled release of an AI system into a real environment for actual use.

It is one step in a larger AI project—not the whole project. A team may discover a workflow, scope a system, integrate it, evaluate it, check readiness, deploy it, support adoption, and monitor what happens in production.

One real example

AI Invoice Review Assistant

1. Test with sample invoices
2. Evaluation passes
3. Release to 5 finance users
4. Monitor failures
5. Improve system
6. Expand access gradually

Monitoring follows deployment because real use reveals failures, edge cases, and workflow friction that tests may not show.
