AI Providers & Models Glossary

Simple English and Chinese explanations for the terms used across this module.

English term中文Simple English中文小白理解
AI ProviderAI 提供商A company or organization that builds or offers AI models and services.开发或提供 AI 模型、产品和服务的公司或组织。
Model Family模型家族A related group of models released under a shared name or lineage.拥有共同名称、来源或技术路线的一组相关模型。
Foundation Model基础模型A broadly trained model that can support many downstream tasks.经过大规模训练、可以支持很多不同任务的基础模型。
Open-weight开放权重Model weights are made available under stated access and license conditions.模型权重可以被获取，但具体使用方式仍要看许可证和条件。
Local Model本地模型A model whose inference runs on hardware you control.推理运行在自己控制的电脑、服务器或其他硬件上。
Cloud Model云端模型A model accessed through infrastructure operated by a provider.通过 AI 提供商管理的云端基础设施来使用模型。
AI GatewayAI 网关A control layer between an application and one or more AI models.位于应用和一个或多个 AI 模型之间，负责路由、可靠性、权限和成本控制的中间层。
Model Routing模型路由Choosing which model should handle a request.根据任务、成本、速度或策略决定某个请求交给哪个模型。
Fallback故障转移 / 备用路径Using another compatible model when the primary path fails.首选模型不可用时，切换到兼容的备用模型。
Inference Server推理服务器A service that runs a model and accepts inference requests.运行模型并接收推理请求的服务。
Runtime运行时Software that executes a model during inference.真正负责执行模型推理的软件环境。
Model vs Product模型 vs 产品A model provides capabilities; a product packages capabilities into a user experience.模型提供能力，产品把模型、界面和流程组合成用户可以使用的体验。
