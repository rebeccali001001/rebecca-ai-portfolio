What is a Scientific Foundation Model?

A scientific foundation model is a broadly trained AI model designed to learn patterns from scientific data and support multiple scientific tasks.

On this pageWhat is it?Think of it like...How it worksReal-world examplesWhat it is NOTRelated conceptsRemember thisVideo

What is it?

A scientific foundation model learns statistical patterns from scientific training data so it can support more than one downstream task.

Scientific data may include protein sequences, molecules, materials, genomic data, scientific text, and other domain-specific representations.

Think of it like...

Think of it like a researcher receiving broad scientific training before specializing in one experiment or prediction task.

A broad base helps the researcher work across related questions, but it does not mean they know every scientific fact or can skip validation.

How it works

1 · Scientific dataCollect domain patternsSequences, structures, measurements, texts, or other scientific representations provide training examples.
2 · Large-scale trainingLearn statistical regularitiesThe model adjusts its internal values to capture patterns in the training data.
3 · General representationsBuild a reusable baseThe learned representations can support several related scientific tasks.
4 · Adapt to a specific taskSpecialize the modelResearchers adapt or prompt the model for a focused prediction, generation, or analysis task.
5 · Validate the resultCheck evidence and limitsExperiments, benchmarks, and expert review test whether the output is useful and reliable.

Real-world examples

Protein structure-related modeling

Learn patterns in protein sequences or structures to support structure prediction and related analysis.

Molecule and drug-candidate modeling

Represent molecules to help compare properties or generate candidate structures for research review.

Materials research

Model relationships among materials, compositions, structures, and measured properties.

Scientific text and data analysis

Organize, compare, summarize, or extract patterns from scientific literature and datasets.

What it is NOT

Scientific model ≠ Scientific database

Scientific model: Produces predictions or representations from learned patterns.

Scientific database: Stores explicit records for lookup and analysis.

Scientific model ≠ LLM

Scientific model: May be trained on domain-specific representations such as sequences, molecules, or measurements.

LLM: Primarily models language tokens, even when it is used for scientific text.

Scientific model ≠ Scientific proof

Scientific model: Offers a prediction, representation, or generated candidate.

Proof or experiment: Provides validation through scientific methods and evidence.
