14 · AI Products & Agents

AI Products & Agents

AI models become useful to people through products such as assistants, coding tools, agents, and computer-use systems.

MODEL+PRODUCT EXPERIENCE+TOOLS= AI PRODUCT

01 · Start here

From model to product

A model provides intelligence. A product packages that intelligence into a usable experience and connects it to a person’s workflow.

AI PROVIDEROpenAIAnthropic · Google
↓
MODEL FAMILYGPTClaude · Gemini
↓
AI PRODUCTChatGPT / CodexClaude App / Claude Code
↓
USER WORKFLOWAsk · build · delegateA useful outcome
MODEL ≠ PRODUCTThe same product may route different tasks to different models. Different products may use the same underlying model.

03 · AI assistants

A useful front door

INPUTQuestion, idea, file, or request
→
PRODUCT EXPERIENCEConversation, context, tools, and settings
→
OUTPUTAnswer, draft, decision, or next step

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, and Perplexity are products people use directly. Their interface, context handling, connected tools, and permissions are part of what makes them different.

Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, Windsurf, and GitHub Copilot connect intelligence to a codebase, tools, validation, and review.

05 · General agents

Agents add action to the loop

USER GOAL“Prepare the best option.”
→
AGENTPlan · decide · act
→
CAPABILITIESTools · browser · computer
→
RESULTDeliverable + review

Computer Use is a capability. It lets a system see and interact with a graphical interface. Browser and desktop agents are environments or product patterns that may use it.

06 · A practical way to choose

Ask what kind of work you want to do

I want to ask, learn, or create.→ Start with an AI assistant
I want to edit and test code.→ Compare coding tools
I want to delegate a broad outcome.→ Explore general agents
I want software to operate a UI.→ Study Computer Use
