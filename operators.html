04 · Operators

Operators perform calculations, comparisons, and logical checks.

On This PageArithmetic OperatorsComparison OperatorsLogical OperatorsMembership OperatorsIdentity Operator

Arithmetic Operators

+ · Addition

total = 10 + 5

Result

15

Also used with strings:

full_name = "Rebecca" + " " + "Li"

- · Subtraction

remaining = 100 - 30

* · Multiplication

price = 8500
quantity = 10
total = price * quantity

Result

85000

/ · Division

average = 100 / 4

Result

25.0

// · Floor division

Returns the whole-number part.

result = 10 // 3

Result

3

% · Remainder

remainder = 10 % 3

Result

1

Common scenario: check whether a number is even:

number % 2 == 0

** · Power

result = 2 ** 3

Result

8

Comparison Operators

Comparison operators return:

True

or

False

== · Equal to

status == "Completed"

!= · Not equal to

status != "Rejected"

> · Greater than

quantity > 100

< · Less than

price < 8000

>= · Greater than or equal to

quantity >= 100

<= · Less than or equal to

price <= 9000

Logical Operators

and

Both conditions must be true.

product == "Copper" and quantity > 10

The product is Copper AND quantity is greater than 10.

or

At least one condition must be true.

product == "Copper" or product == "Aluminum"

not

Reverses a condition.

not is_completed

Membership Operators

in

Checks whether a value exists inside a collection.

products = ["Copper", "Aluminum", "Nickel"]
"Copper" in products

Result

True

not in

"Gold" not in products

Result

True

Identity Operator

is

is checks whether two references point to the same object.

For beginners, it is commonly seen with:

is None
delivery_date is None

Meaning: Check whether delivery_date currently has no value.

Recommended:

if delivery_date is None:
    print("No delivery date")

Do not encourage:

delivery_date == None
