03 · Tokens, Context & Inference

What is a Token?

A token is a small unit of text that an AI model reads and processes. It can be a whole word, part of a word, punctuation, or even a space.

Token detail

Language models do not read a paragraph exactly as humans do. They break text into smaller pieces, then process those pieces as tokens.

Important nuance

A tokenizer does not always split text neatly by whole words. For example, unbelievable may become several tokens depending on the model and tokenizer.

Think of tokens like LEGO blocks: small pieces combine to build a larger idea.

Why it matters

More tokens can mean more context, more processing, and potentially more cost.

context = instructions + history + retrieved text + task + output

Key takeaway

A token is a small unit of text processed by an AI model.
