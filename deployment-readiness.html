08 · Evaluation & Reliability

Deployment Readiness

A deployment is ready when the workflow has evidence, ownership, safeguards, and a rollback or human fallback—not just a promising demo.

Readiness gates

Capability
Representative tasks pass the quality bar.
Reliability
Failures are detected and recoverable.
Operations
Latency, cost, monitoring, and ownership are defined.

Detail

  • Version the prompt, model, tools, and eval set.
  • Define approval and escalation paths.
  • Log inputs, outputs, tool calls, and latency safely.
  • Document rollback, fallback, and support ownership.

Sample

A settlement assistant can launch in shadow mode first: produce recommendations, log evidence, and let an operator approve actions until the scorecard is stable.
