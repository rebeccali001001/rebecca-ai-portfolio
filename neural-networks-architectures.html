02 · Neural Networks & Model Architectures

How Modern Neural Models Are Built

This module explains how neural networks are structured, what layers do, and how major architectures such as CNNs, RNNs, Transformers, Attention-based models, and Diffusion Models fit into the bigger picture.

Learn the building blocks and families of modern neural model architectures.

The Big Picture · architecture family map

Building blocks, families, and mechanisms

NEURAL NETWORKSModels made of connected computational units
↓ LAYERSRepeated processing stages inside the network
COMMON ARCHITECTURE FAMILIES├── CNN · grid-like data such as images
├── RNN / LSTM · sequences processed step by step
└── Transformer · relationships across tokens
ATTENTIONA key mechanism used inside Transformers; attention is not the same thing as a Transformer.

Knowledge Structure

Organize the ideas by role

A · BUILDING BLOCKS

Neural Networks

Models made of connected computational units.

Layers

Repeated processing stages inside the network.

B · ARCHITECTURES

CNN

Often used for grid-like data such as images.

RNN / LSTM

Designed to process sequences step by step.

Transformer

Processes relationships across tokens with attention.

C · MECHANISM

Attention

Helps a model weigh which parts of input matter most.

D · GENERATIVE FAMILY

Diffusion Models

Generate data by learning to reverse a noising process.

Key Distinctions

Related concepts are not interchangeable

Neural NetworkOne type of model structure≠Deep LearningBroader approach that often uses multi-layer networks
AttentionMechanism≠TransformerFull architecture that uses attention
CNNModel architecture≠Computer VisionBroader application area
Diffusion ModelOne model family≠Generative AIBroader capability area
