09 · Enterprise AI Deployment

Make AI fit the work people already do.

Enterprise deployment is a connected path: understand the workflow, define a safe pilot, connect the right systems, prove the outcome, and help people adopt it.

Mind map

Each topic answers one practical question. Open a card for the plain-English explanation, sample, and video.

Sample · opening a neighborhood lunch counter

One everyday story connects all five topics

Imagine a lunch counter where orders are slow and mistakes are common. Before adding a clever assistant, the team follows one order from the queue to the customer, chooses one narrow improvement, connects the menu and ticket systems, taste-tests normal and unusual orders, and teaches the staff how to use the new helper while keeping a manual fallback.

01 · DiscoverWatch the queueFind the real bottleneck and the moments people improvise.
02 · ScopeChoose one dishDefine a small pilot with one input, outcome, owner, and limit.
03 · IntegrateConnect ticket to kitchenPass the right order data through existing tools and approvals.
04 · EvaluateTaste-test the resultCheck normal orders, edge cases, timing, and recovery.
05 · AdoptTeach the teamRoll out with feedback, clear ownership, and a manual fallback.
