07 · Lists

Group related items, steps, and terms into meaningful lists.

What is it?

Lists express a collection of related items. Think of it like: a checklist, recipe, or glossary.

Basic Syntax

<ul><li>Design</li><li>Build</li></ul>
<ol><li>Sign in</li><li>Confirm</li></ol>
<dl><dt>HTML</dt><dd>Page structure</dd></dl>

Common Examples

Use ul for unordered items, ol where order matters, and dl/dt/dd for term-definition pairs. Nest a list inside an li for hierarchy.

Common Mistakes

Do not create a list by typing dashes or numbers into paragraphs. Use a list for navigation, steps, features, and collections.

Quick Reference

ul unordered collection · ol ordered steps · li an item · dl terms and definitions.
