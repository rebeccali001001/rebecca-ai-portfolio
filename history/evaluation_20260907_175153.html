08 · Evaluation & Reliability

Know whether the system works.

This chapter moves from a working demo to repeatable evidence, named failure modes, and a responsible release decision.

Why this matters

AI systems can sound convincing while failing on the cases that matter. Evaluation gives a team a shared way to test behavior, explain risk, and decide what to improve before release.
