08 · Evaluation & Reliability

What is AI Evaluation?

AI evaluation measures how well a model or AI system performs for a defined task.

ON THIS PAGEWhat is it?Think of it like...How it worksExamplesWhat it is NOTRelated conceptsRemember thisVideo
01 / START HERE

What is it?

Evaluation starts with a task, test examples, expected behavior, and a way to judge the result.

TaskWhat should it do?
ExamplesWhat should we test?
ExpectedWhat counts as right?
CriteriaHow will we score it?

Then run the system, compare the result, and measure performance.

Depending on the task, evaluation can look at accuracy, relevance, groundedness, format correctness, latency, cost, safety, or human preference. You do not need every metric—only the ones that help answer the real question.

02 / A USEFUL MENTAL MODEL

Think of it like... An Exam With a Marking Rubric

Think of evaluation like an exam with a clear grading rubric.

You do not simply ask:

“Does this answer look good?”

You define:

  • what counts as correct
  • what matters most
  • how results are scored

That makes quality discussable, repeatable, and useful for improvement.

05 / CLEAR UP THE CONFUSION

What it is NOT

≠ Benchmark

Evaluation can be specific to your real task.

Benchmark usually uses a standardized public or fixed test set.

≠ Testing Only the Model

Evaluation may measure the complete system: retrieval, tools, prompts, and workflows—not just the model.

≠ One Score

AI quality often requires several metrics and sometimes human review.
