LLMs & Transformers

Understand how language models learn patterns, process context, and generate text.

Chapter introduction

From broad training to next-token prediction

This chapter separates the model architecture, the learned values, the training stages, and the language behavior that people see in an LLM.

Why this matters

What this chapter helps you understand

The terms LLM, Transformer, parameters, pre-training, and fine-tuning describe different layers of the same ecosystem. Keeping them separate prevents common misunderstandings.

Concept map

How the pieces connect

Broad data\n↓\nPre-training\n↓\nParameters are learned\n↓\nTransformer processes token relationships\n↓\nLLM predicts the next token\n↓\nFine-tuning can adapt behavior

These are related stages and components, not interchangeable names for one thing.
