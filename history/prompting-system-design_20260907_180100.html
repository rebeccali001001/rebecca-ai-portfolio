04 · Prompting & System Design

Prompting & System Design

Make the model’s job clear, checkable, and recoverable.

Chapter introduction

Design the contract around the model

This chapter connects standing instructions, predictable handoffs, and safe behavior when an AI step does not work.

Why this matters

What this chapter helps you understand

A useful AI feature is more than a clever prompt. It needs clear responsibilities, a usable output shape, and a plan for uncertainty or failure.

Concept map

Three layers around the model

System Prompt\n↓\nUser task + context\n↓\nModel response\n↓\nStructured Output\n↓\nValidation\n↓\nFailure Handling / human fallback

The concepts support one another, but they solve different design problems.
