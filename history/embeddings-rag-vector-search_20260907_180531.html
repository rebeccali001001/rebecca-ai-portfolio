05 · Embeddings, RAG & Vector Search

One evidence pipeline

This chapter explains how content becomes searchable by meaning, how relevant evidence is retrieved, and how an AI answer can stay tied to a source.

Why this matters

Useful answers need useful evidence

A model may know broad patterns, but a reliable application often needs current, private, or traceable information from an approved source.
