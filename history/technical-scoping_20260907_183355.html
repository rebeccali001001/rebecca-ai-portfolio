Technical Scoping

Turn a promising idea into a small, testable system.

Definition

Technical scoping sets the boundaries of an AI job: inputs, outputs, model capability, data sources, integrations, constraints, safety checks, owners, and measurable success criteria.

In plain English

It changes “use AI for invoices” into “for this document type, extract these fields, cite the source, validate the result, and send low-confidence cases to a named reviewer.”

Key points

  • Define the job: one input, outcome, and stopping condition.
  • Choose the capability: extraction, classification, retrieval, drafting, or action.
  • Set the boundary: list what the system must not do.
  • Plan the fallback: make uncertainty and human review explicit.

Application

Use a scope note to align product, domain, security, data, and engineering teams before a pilot. It gives integration and evaluation a concrete target.

A good scope makes tradeoffs visible before implementation effort becomes expensive.

Sample · plan the lunch counter

Before buying an appliance for the lunch counter, define one pilot: recognize the lunch order, check the ingredient list, and print a kitchen ticket. Do not promise full payment automation, substitutions without approval, or support for every menu item on day one.

OutputOne kitchen ticketThe result is useful and testable.
