01 · Start here

What is it?

A model can generate plausible text from learned patterns. Grounding gives it specific evidence to work from—so the answer is connected to the world of the current task.

05 · Clear up the vocabulary

What it is NOT

Grounding ≠ RAG

Grounding: the goal of tying answers to external evidence.

RAG: one common architecture used to achieve it.

Grounding ≠ Fine-tuning

Grounding: adds information at runtime.

Fine-tuning: changes model behavior through training.

Grounding ≠ guaranteed truth

Grounding: gives the model better evidence.

It does not guarantee perfect interpretation or reasoning.
