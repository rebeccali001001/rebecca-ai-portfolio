Evaluation & Reliability

This chapter explains how to test AI behavior, name repeatable failures, and decide whether a system is ready for a real use case.

Why this matters

A convincing demo is not enough

AI output can vary and systems can fail outside the happy path. Evaluation turns expectations into evidence that people can inspect.
