Evaluation & Reliability

Test whether an AI feature works, fails safely, and is ready to operate.

Chapter introduction

Prove the workflow before scaling it

This chapter treats AI evaluation as a system question. A useful answer is only one part; the workflow must also handle failures, latency, permissions, and human fallback.

Why this matters

What this chapter helps you understand

Evaluation turns “the demo looks good” into evidence that a defined workflow meets a quality bar under representative conditions.
