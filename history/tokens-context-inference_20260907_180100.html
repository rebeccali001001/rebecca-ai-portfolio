Tokens, Context & Inference

Understand what a model reads, how much it can use at once, and why output can feel fast or slow.

Chapter introduction

How an AI request moves

This chapter follows one request from text, to tokens, to available context, through inference, and into a streamed response.

Why this matters

What this chapter helps you understand

Tokens, context windows, KV cache, latency, and tokens per second describe different parts of the same request. They should not be treated as synonyms.

Concept map
