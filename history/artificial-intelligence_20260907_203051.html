What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence is technology that allows computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

What is it?

AI is the broad field of creating computer systems that can recognize patterns, make predictions, generate content, make recommendations, or take actions.

Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI are different approaches or areas within AI.

Think of it like...

Think of AI like a student who has studied millions of examples. When you give it something new, it looks for patterns similar to what it has seen before and produces a useful result.

PredictionWhat may happen next?
RecommendationWhat may be useful?
GenerationWhat content can be created?
ActionWhat should happen next?

How it works

1 · InputReceive informationA photo, document, message, or sensor reading arrives.
2 · ProcessFind patternsRules or learned patterns are applied.
3 · Decide or generateCreate a resultThe system predicts, recommends, classifies, answers, or creates.
4 · OutputReturn the resultIt goes to the user or another system.
5 · ReviewCheck when neededA person verifies important or uncertain results.

Real-world examples

Everyday example · Photo recognition

Input: a photo. AI: recognizes visual patterns. Output: a face, object, or scene label.

Business example · Invoice processing

Input: an invoice. AI: reads fields and flags unusual values. Output: structured data and exceptions for review.

What it is NOT

AI ≠ ChatGPT

AI: A broad field of computer systems that perform tasks associated with intelligence.

ChatGPT: One AI product.

AI ≠ Fixed-rule automation

Automation: Can simply follow predefined if/then rules.

AI: Often uses pattern recognition, prediction, classification, or generation.

AI ≠ The human mind

AI: Can perform tasks that appear intelligent.

The human mind: AI does not think or understand exactly like a human.
