AI Foundations

Understand the five ideas behind modern AI.

Chapter introduction

This chapter explains how Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Foundation Models, and Generative AI fit together.

If you understand these five ideas, many later AI concepts become much easier to understand.

The Big Picture

These ideas are related, but they are not one strict line. AI is the broad field; Machine Learning is one approach within it, and Deep Learning is one type of Machine Learning.

01 · UMBRELLAArtificial IntelligenceThe broad field of making computers perform tasks that appear intelligent. 02 · APPROACHMachine LearningA way for computers to learn patterns from examples.
AI FoundationsFive connected ideas, not a single pipeline
03 · TYPE OF MLDeep LearningA type of machine learning that uses many neural-network layers. 04 · BUILT WITH DLFoundation ModelsLarge broadly trained models that can be reused for many tasks. 05 · CREATESGenerative AIAI that creates new text, images, audio, video, code, or other content.

How to read this: Foundation Models are usually built with Deep Learning and trained broadly for many tasks. Many modern Generative AI systems use Foundation Models, but Generative AI is defined by what it creates, not by one specific architecture.

One simple story

One story that connects the five ideas

Imagine teaching a helpful kitchen robot. Each step is a learning analogy.

01 · AIGive it a goalHelp with a useful kitchen task.
02 · Machine LearningLearn from examplesShow it examples of requests and recipes.
03 · Deep LearningRecognize patternsUse layered networks to spot complex patterns.
04 · Foundation ModelStart broadly trainedReuse a model that already knows many patterns.
05 · Generative AICreate a resultDraft a new recipe from what is available.

This story is only a learning analogy; real AI systems can use different architectures.
