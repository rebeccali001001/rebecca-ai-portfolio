09 · Enterprise AI Deployment

How AI Moves from Demo to Real Business Use

This chapter explains how an AI idea becomes a working system inside a real organization.

You will learn how to identify the right workflow, define technical boundaries, connect systems, evaluate the solution, and help people actually use it.

Chapter Introduction

From a clever demo to a dependable workflow

Enterprise AI is not one launch moment. It is a connected set of decisions that turns a useful idea into something people can trust and use.

Core Concepts

The six building blocks

Workflow DiscoveryFind the real process, friction, and opportunity.Technical ScopingSet clear boundaries for data, actions, and risk.IntegrationConnect the AI system to the tools around it.EvaluationCollect evidence that the system works.AdoptionMake the new workflow useful in daily work.
