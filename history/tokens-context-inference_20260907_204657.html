03 · Tokens, Context & Inference

How an LLM Turns Your Input into an Answer

This chapter explains what happens between typing a prompt and receiving an answer from a language model.

You will learn how text becomes tokens, how much information the model can see, how generation happens, and why some models respond faster than others.

One request, step by step

“Explain AI in simple English.”

Step 1 · TextThe user types a sentence.
Step 2 · TokensThe tokenizer splits it into smaller units.
Step 3 · ContextThe tokens enter the model’s available context.
Step 4 · InferenceThe model calculates likely next tokens.
Step 5 · GenerationTokens are generated one by one.
Step 6 · OutputThe generated tokens are converted back into readable text.
KV Cachereuses information from earlier tokens
