09 · Enterprise AI Deployment

Move from a promising idea to a supported workflow.

This chapter explains the delivery work around an AI feature: choose the workflow, scope the system, connect it safely, evaluate it, and help people adopt it.

Why this matters

A useful model is only one part of an enterprise system. The workflow, integrations, evidence, ownership, and user trust determine whether the feature creates value in daily work.
