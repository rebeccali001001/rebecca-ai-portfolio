What is it?

Artificial Intelligence is technology that allows computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

AI is the broad field of building systems that recognize patterns, make predictions, generate content, recommend actions, or act on information. It includes learned systems and systems built from explicit rules.

Think of it like...

Think of AI like a student who has studied many examples and instructions. When a new problem arrives, it uses patterns or rules to produce a result. This is an analogy: AI does not think or understand exactly like a person.

How it works

1 · InputReceive informationA photo, document, message, or sensor reading arrives.
2 · ProcessApply rules or patternsThe system processes the input with programmed rules or learned patterns.
3 · DecideEstimate a resultIt classifies, predicts, recommends, or selects an action.
4 · OutputReturn a resultThe result goes to a person or another system.

Real-world examples

Everyday example · Photo recognition

Input: A photo

System: Recognizes visual patterns

Output: A face, object, or scene label

Business example · Invoice processing

Input: An invoice

System: Reads fields and flags unusual values

Output: Structured data and exceptions for review

What it is NOT

AI ≠ ChatGPT

AI: The broad field of computer systems that perform tasks associated with intelligence.

ChatGPT: One AI product built from models and other software.

AI ≠ Fixed-rule automation

AI: May use learned patterns, predictions, or generation.

Fixed-rule automation: Follows predefined instructions such as if/then rules.

AI ≠ The human mind

AI: Can perform selected tasks that appear intelligent.

The human mind: Is not reproduced exactly by AI.

Related concepts

Artificial Intelligence includes Machine Learning which includes Deep Learning
Foundation Models are broadly trained models often built with deep learning. Generative AI describes systems that create new content.
