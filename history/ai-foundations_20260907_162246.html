Sample · teaching a helpful kitchen robot

Imagine one kitchen helper learning to turn a person’s request into a safe, useful recipe. Each step below names the topic it represents.

01 · Artificial IntelligenceSet the goalMake the robot understand a request and help with a kitchen task.
02 · Machine LearningLearn from examplesShow it recipe cards, ingredients, actions, and outcomes.
03 · Deep LearningRecognize patternsUse layered representations to see a tomato, pan, or spoken request.
04 · Foundation ModelsBring broad knowledgeGive it a reusable base for language, instructions, and many domains.
