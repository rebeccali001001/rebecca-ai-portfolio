What is it?

Fine-tuning is additional training that adapts a pre-trained model for a more specific behavior, style, format, or task.

Instead of training from scratch, fine-tuning starts with an existing base model and continues training on a smaller set of targeted examples.

Think of it like...

Pre-training is like general education.

Fine-tuning is like later specialist training for one job.

The person does not relearn everything from zero; they build on what they already know.

How it works

1 · Base modelStart with a pre-trained model.
2 · Training examplesPrepare examples of the desired behavior.
3 · Additional trainingUpdate selected model parameters or adapters.
4 · Specialized modelThe model becomes more consistent for the target behavior.
5 · EvaluationTest whether behavior improved without regressions.

Real-world examples

Support classification

Input: Customer ticket.
Fine-tuning: Examples show the correct category labels.
Result: More consistent classification.

Writing style

Input: Draft request.
Fine-tuning: Examples demonstrate a preferred tone and format.
Result: More consistent output style.

Structured output

Input: Document.
Fine-tuning: Examples demonstrate a fixed output schema.
Result: More consistent structured responses.

What it is NOT

Fine-tuning≠Pre-training

Pre-training: Broad initial training on large-scale data.
Fine-tuning: Later targeted training on a smaller, focused dataset.

Fine-tuning≠Prompting

Prompting: Changes runtime instructions.
Fine-tuning: Changes learned model behavior through training.

Fine-tuning≠RAG

RAG: Retrieves external knowledge at runtime.
Fine-tuning: Changes model behavior through additional training.

Related concepts

Pre-training ↓ Base Model ↓ Fine-tuning ↓ Specialized behavior

For a specific application, Prompting, RAG, and Fine-tuning are different adaptation methods—not strict levels in one hierarchy.

Transcript

Fine-tuning is additional training after a model has learned broad language patterns. Like professional training after general education, curated examples teach a stable task, format, or style. The model is then evaluated for quality, edge cases, and safety. Fine-tuning changes behavior; it does not guarantee truth or replace retrieval for fresh facts.
