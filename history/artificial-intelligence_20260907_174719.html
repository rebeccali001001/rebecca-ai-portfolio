What is it?

Artificial Intelligence is technology that allows computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

AI is the broad field of creating computer systems that recognize patterns, make predictions, generate content, make recommendations, or take actions. Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI are different approaches or areas within AI.

Think of it like...

Think of AI like a student who has studied many examples. When a new problem arrives, it uses learned patterns or written rules to produce a useful result. This is an analogy: AI does not think or understand exactly like a person.

How it works

1 · InputReceive informationA photo, document, message, or sensor reading arrives.
2 · ProcessApply rules or patternsThe system uses programmed rules or learned patterns.
3 · Decide or generateCreate a resultIt predicts, classifies, recommends, answers, or creates.
4 · OutputReturn the resultThe result goes to a person or another system.
5 · ReviewCheck when neededA person verifies important or uncertain results.

Real-world examples

Everyday example · Photo recognition

Input: a photo

AI / System: recognizes visual patterns

Output: a face, object, or scene label

Business example · Invoice processing

Input: an invoice

AI / System: reads fields and flags unusual values

Output: structured data and exceptions for review

What it is NOT

AI ≠ ChatGPT

AI: a broad field of computer systems

ChatGPT: one AI product

AI ≠ Fixed-rule automation

AI: can use learned patterns, predictions, or generation

Automation: can simply follow predefined if/then rules

AI ≠ The human mind

AI: can perform tasks that appear intelligent

The human mind: is not reproduced exactly by AI
