LLMs & Transformers

This chapter explains how a Large Language Model learns language patterns, stores what it learns, processes context, and can later be adapted for specific behavior.

If you understand these five ideas, later topics such as Tokens, Context Window, RAG, Agents, and local models become much easier to understand.

The big picture

Five ideas, with different jobs

These concepts are connected, but they are not five interchangeable parts.

One simple story

Teaching a new helper at a tea shop

A small analogy to make the relationships easier to remember.

LLMThe helper can continue and generate language.
TransformerThe helper looks at the whole order and connects relevant words.
ParametersThe helper keeps learned tendencies as internal settings.
Pre-trainingThe helper first studies many general examples.
Fine-tuningLater, the helper practices this tea shop’s specific style.

This is only a learning analogy, not a literal description of how the model works.
