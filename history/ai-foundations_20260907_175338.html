AI Foundations

Understand the five ideas behind modern AI.

Chapter introduction

From a broad field to useful systems

This chapter explains how Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Foundation Models, and Generative AI fit together. They are related, but they are not one strict pipeline.

Why this matters

What this chapter helps you understand

These boundaries help you understand later topics without treating every model, product, or AI feature as the same thing.

Concept map

The big picture

Artificial Intelligence\n├── Machine Learning\n│ └── Deep Learning\n├── Foundation Models\n└── Generative AI

Machine Learning is one approach within AI. Deep Learning is one type of Machine Learning. Foundation Models are broadly trained models, and Generative AI is defined by creating new content.
