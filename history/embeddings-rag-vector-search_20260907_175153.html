Embeddings, RAG & Vector Search

Understand how AI systems represent meaning, find evidence, and keep answers tied to sources.

Chapter introduction

One evidence pipeline

This chapter connects embeddings, vector search, RAG, and grounding. Each concept has a different job in the path from a question to a supported answer.

Why this matters

What this chapter helps you understand

A reliable retrieval system needs more than a search box. It needs a representation, a retrieval method, a way to place evidence into context, and a check that the answer is supported.

Concept map

Question to evidence-backed answer

Question\n↓\nEmbedding or keyword query\n↓\nVector Search\n↓\nRetrieved passages\n↓\nContext\n↓\nLLM answer\n↓\nGrounding check

RAG is the overall retrieval-plus-generation pattern. Embeddings and Vector Search are possible components. Grounding describes the evidence relationship.
