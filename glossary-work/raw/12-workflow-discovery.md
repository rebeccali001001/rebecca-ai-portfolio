## Potential Missing Concepts

The page establishes a high-level discovery workflow but does not fully define the following useful adjacent concepts. They are retained as review candidates, not presented as page-defined facts.

- **process mining**：从系统事件日志重建和分析真实流程；与 workflow discovery 相关，但本页没有提及事件日志或自动化流程挖掘。
- **business process management (BPM)**：管理、建模、改进和治理业务流程的完整 discipline。
- **BPMN (Business Process Model and Notation)**：用于绘制标准业务流程图的表示法。
- **service blueprint**：同时展示用户、前台、后台和支持系统如何协作的服务流程图。
- **value stream mapping**：从价值交付角度识别步骤、等待和浪费的流程分析方法。
- **stakeholder mapping**：识别流程中参与者、受影响者和责任人的方法。
- **persona**：代表某类用户、角色或工作者的典型画像。
- **role / responsibility**：流程中谁负责执行、批准、复核或接收交接。
- **RACI**：区分 Responsible、Accountable、Consulted、Informed 的责任分配框架。
- **event log**：记录工作流事件、时间和对象的系统日志。
- **process variant**：同一流程因条件不同而出现的实际路径变体。
- **happy path**：没有异常、按预期顺利完成的标准路径。
- **edge case**：很少见但可能暴露系统边界的特殊案例。
- **case volume**：单位时间进入流程的案例数量。
- **cycle time**：一个案例从开始到完成所需的总时间。
- **wait time**：工作在交接、审批或队列中等待的时间。
- **touch time**：人员或系统实际处理案例的时间，不含等待。
- **throughput**：单位时间内完成的案例数量。
- **queue**：等待处理的工单、文件或任务集合。
- **backlog**：已经进入流程但尚未完成的工作积压。
- **rework**：因为错误或缺失信息而重复处理同一工作。
- **defect rate**：结果中出现错误或不合格项的比例。
- **handoff failure**：交接时信息丢失、责任不清或任务未继续的失败。
- **root cause analysis**：追查摩擦、延迟或错误背后根因的方法。
- **5 Whys**：通过连续追问“为什么”寻找问题根因的分析 technique。
- **constraint**：限制流程设计或 AI 使用的条件，如权限、成本、法规或数据。
- **business rule**：指导分类、审批、路由或例外处理的业务规则。
- **decision table**：按条件和结果系统化表达决策规则的表格。
- **automation candidate**：经过筛选、适合考虑自动化的流程步骤。
- **AI use case**：明确的 AI 使用场景、用户、输入、输出和价值假设。
- **use-case hypothesis**：关于 AI 能在某个流程中带来什么价值的可验证假设。
- **human-in-the-loop design**：把人工检查、批准、接管和停止点设计进流程。
- **human handoff**：将无法安全自动处理的案例转交人工。
- **approval gate**：高风险动作执行前必须通过的批准节点。
- **escalation path**：异常、风险或超出权限时升级给更高层级或人工的路径。
- **fallback**：AI 不可靠或不可用时采用的备用路径。
- **guardrail**：限制危险输出、动作或访问范围的安全控制。
- **permissions**：规定系统、用户或 AI 可以访问什么和执行什么的权限边界。
- **audit trail**：记录输入、决定、交接、批准和输出的可追溯记录。
- **observability**：让流程运行状态、失败和瓶颈能够被记录和诊断。
- **data lineage**：追踪数据从来源、加工到输出的流转路径。
- **data contract**：约定输入输出字段、格式、质量和责任的接口契约。
- **integration point**：工作流与另一个系统、API 或数据源连接的位置。
- **API**：让系统以程序化方式交换数据或触发动作的接口。
- **structured data**：按字段和固定结构组织的数据。
- **unstructured data**：文本、文件、图片等没有固定字段结构的数据。
- **workflow orchestration**：协调多个步骤、系统、重试和人工节点的运行机制。
- **process owner**：对流程整体结果、规则和改进负责的人或团队。
- **operating model**：规定人员、流程、系统和治理如何协作的运行模式。
- **technical requirements**：从业务流程和需求推导出的技术要求。
- **acceptance criteria**：判断原型或系统是否达到要求的验收标准。
- **success metric**：衡量流程改进是否产生价值的指标。
- **baseline**：改进或自动化前的原始表现，用于比较后续变化。
- **ROI (return on investment)**：比较投入成本与产生收益的投资回报。
- **pilot**：在有限团队、范围或时间内进行的试点。
- **MVP (minimum viable product)**：用于验证核心价值的最小可用版本。
- **process redesign**：基于发现结果重新设计流程，而不只是自动化现有步骤。
- **continuous improvement**：根据反馈和数据持续改进工作流的循环。

## Do Not Confuse Candidates

- **Workflow Discovery vs Brainstorming**：Workflow Discovery studies real work and evidence; Brainstorming generates possible ideas. 一个研究现实，一个发散想法。
- **Workflow Discovery vs Technical Scoping**：Workflow Discovery explains the current process and needs; Technical Scoping defines a feasible system boundary. 前者先理解业务，后者再界定技术。
- **Workflow Discovery vs Process Automation**：Discovery maps and assesses the process; Process Automation changes how the process runs. 发现流程不等于已经自动化。
- **Workflow Discovery vs Workflow Design**：Discovery describes what happens today; design chooses how the workflow should work in the future.
- **Workflow Discovery vs Process Mining**：Discovery can use interviews and observation; Process Mining specifically uses event data and logs to analyze process behavior.
- **Real Work vs Ideal Process**：Real Work is what people actually do, including workarounds and exceptions; an ideal process is what documentation says should happen.
- **Workflow Map vs Workflow**：A workflow is the actual sequence of work; a workflow map is a representation of that sequence.
- **Process vs Workflow**：Process can describe a broader repeatable business method; workflow emphasizes the connected movement of tasks, information, people, and systems.
- **Step vs Decision Point**：A step performs an activity; a decision point selects a path based on a condition or judgment.
- **Handoff vs Decision**：A handoff changes the owner of work; a decision changes what path or action follows.
- **Handoff vs Integration**：A handoff transfers responsibility; integration connects systems so information or actions can move between them.
- **Delay vs Bottleneck**：A delay is time lost at a point; a bottleneck is a constrained point that limits overall flow or capacity.
- **Friction vs Error**：Friction makes work difficult, slow, or repetitive; an error is an incorrect action, decision, or result.
- **Error Point vs Exception Point**：An error point is likely to produce a mistake; an exception point is a case that legitimately needs a non-standard path.
- **Exception vs Edge Case**：An exception is a deviation from the normal process; an edge case is an uncommon boundary condition, and it may or may not require a separate business path.
- **Input vs Data**：Data is the information used or produced; input is the part entering a particular workflow or system step.
- **Input vs Output**：Input enters a step; output leaves it as a result.
- **AI System vs AI Model**：An AI system can include a model, interface, rules, data, integrations, and human controls; a model is only one component.
- **AI Opportunity vs AI Solution**：An opportunity is a possible place for value; a solution is a proposed design for addressing it.
- **AI Fit vs Feasibility**：AI fit asks whether the work suits AI; feasibility asks whether the proposed implementation can actually be built and operated.
- **AI Fit vs Automation**：A workflow may be a good AI candidate without being fully automatable; fit includes quality, risk, data, and human-control considerations.
- **Human Review vs Human Control**：Human review is one checking activity; human control is the broader ability to approve, intervene, override, or stop.
- **Human Review vs Human Handoff**：Review checks a result; handoff transfers responsibility for handling the case.
- **Human-in-the-Loop vs Human-on-the-Loop**：Human-in-the-loop places people inside the decision flow; human-on-the-loop emphasizes supervision over an operating system.
- **Quality vs Correctness**：Correctness asks whether an answer or action is right; quality is broader and can include completeness, usefulness, and consistency.
- **Risk vs Error**：An error is something that went wrong; risk is the possibility and consequence of something going wrong.
- **Data Availability vs Data Quality**：Availability asks whether data can be accessed; quality asks whether that data is accurate, complete, consistent, and usable.
- **Business Rule vs AI Judgment**：A business rule is explicitly defined; an AI judgment is produced from a model or learned behavior and may require review.
- **Prototype vs Production System**：A prototype tests an idea in a limited scope; a production system must operate reliably in real work.
- **Prototype vs Pilot**：A prototype tests feasibility or interaction; a pilot tests a solution with a limited real user group or workflow.
- **Prototype vs MVP**：A prototype may be non-production and exploratory; an MVP is the smallest usable product intended to validate core value.
- **Integrate vs Automate**：Integration connects systems; automation changes or executes work with less manual effort. Integration may be needed without full automation.
- **Investigate vs Assess**：Assessment makes a judgment from current evidence; investigation gathers more evidence when the current evidence is insufficient.
- **Scope vs Technical Scope**：Scope chooses the boundary of the next work; technical scope specifies the boundary of the implementable system.
- **Process Automation vs Workflow Automation**：The terms are often used similarly; process automation can refer to broader business-process change, while workflow automation often emphasizes step orchestration.
- **Ticket Triage vs Ticket Routing**：Triage determines category, priority, or handling need; routing sends the ticket to an owner or queue.
- **Document Review vs Field Extraction**：Document review is the broader checking task; field extraction is one capability within it.
- **Missing Information vs Exception**：Missing information is a specific deficiency; an exception is any case that follows a non-standard path.
- **Related Concept vs Page Definition**：A linked concept such as Integration or Evaluation is related to Workflow Discovery but is not fully defined on this page.
- **Real-World Example vs General Definition**：An example illustrates one workflow; the definition describes the concept across workflows.
- **Workflow Discovery vs Evaluation**：Discovery finds and scopes the problem; Evaluation measures whether the resulting system meets expectations.
- **Workflow Discovery vs Adoption**：Discovery understands the work before a solution is designed; Adoption concerns whether people later use the solution in real work.
- **Workflow Discovery vs Integration**：Discovery maps what needs to happen; Integration connects the chosen system to existing systems.
- **Automation Opportunity vs Guaranteed Automation**：An opportunity is a candidate for investigation, not proof that automation will be safe or valuable.
- **One Workflow Map vs Complete Reality**：A map is a useful abstraction; it may omit hidden work, rare exceptions, informal decisions, or changing conditions unless discovery looks for them.
