What is Audio Generation?

Audio generation uses AI models to create new sound, speech, music, or other audio.

On this pageWhat is it?Think of it like...How it worksReal-world examplesWhat it is NOTRelated conceptsRemember thisVideo

What is it?

Audio generation is the use of AI to create new sound.

Inputs may include text, audio, music structure, a voice reference, or other conditions. Outputs may include speech, music, sound effects, or ambient audio.

Think of it like...

Think of it like giving a digital sound studio a description.

The studio uses your request and sound clues to produce audio matching it.

How it works

1 · ConditionGive an inputText, audio, structure, or another condition.
2 · EncodeRepresent the inputTurn the condition into information the model can use.
3 · GenerateCreate a representationThe model builds new audio information.
4 · DecodeMake a waveformA decoder turns it into playable audio.
5 · OutputReturn the resultSpeech, music, effects, or ambience.

Real-world examples

Text-to-speech

Input: written text

Output: spoken narration

Music generation

Input: style, mood, or prompt

Output: a song or instrumental track

Sound effects

Input: “rain on a window”

Output: matching sound or ambience

What it is NOT

Audio Generation ≠ Speech Recognition

Generation: creates new audio.

Recognition: turns speech into text or meaning.

Audio Generation ≠ TTS

Generation: includes many kinds of sound.

TTS: one speech-generation use case.

Audio Generation ≠ Music Generation Only

Generation: covers speech, effects, and ambience too.

Music generation: focuses on musical audio.

Related concepts

Generative AI → Audio Generation → Speech · Music · Effects
Audio generation is one modality of generative AI.
