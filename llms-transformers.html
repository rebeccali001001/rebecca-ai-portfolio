One everyday sample

Teaching a new helper at a tea shop

Imagine a tea shop training a new helper. The helper learns from many orders, pays attention to the whole request, remembers patterns as adjustable settings, and then practices this shop’s exact way of working.

1 · LLMThe helper predicts what the customer is likely asking for next.
2 · TransformerThe helper reads the full order so “it” connects to the right drink.
3 · ParametersThe helper’s learned recipe knobs store useful patterns.
4 · Pre-trainingMany books and practice orders build broad language ability.
5 · Fine-tuningShop-specific examples teach the exact format and tone.
How to use this module

Start with the model, end with the task

Read the topics as a chain: the LLM is the prediction engine; the Transformer is the architecture; parameters hold learned patterns; pre-training builds broad capability; fine-tuning specializes behavior. In a real deployment, test the complete chain against the task, latency, privacy, and changing knowledge requirements.
