Product Services

Modern applications often use specialized services for payments, email, messaging, media, search, maps, analytics, and monitoring.

Modern products rarely build every capability themselves. They connect specialized external services.

Why use external services?

Instead of building payment infrastructure, email delivery, maps, search, or analytics from scratch, a product integrates specialized providers.

FASTER DEVELOPMENTStart with proven building blocks.
SPECIALIZED INFRASTRUCTUREUse systems built for difficult operational work.
LESS CUSTOM MAINTENANCEKeep your team focused on the product.

TRADE-OFFS

Cost · vendor dependency

ALSO CONSIDER

Data / privacy · integration complexity
